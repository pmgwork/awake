This is a beta release. To receive it in-app, enable **Settings → General → Receive Beta Updates**.

## What's Changed Since Beta 2

- Fixed the Mac still going to sleep when the lid was closed during Keep Awake, especially after unplugging the power adapter or a display with the lid closed. macOS could clear the lid-close sleep override at that moment and put the Mac to sleep before the helper reapplied it. While the lid is closed, the helper now also turns on the macOS `disablesleep` power setting, and turns it off again when you open the lid or Keep Awake stops. If the helper can't turn it off, for example after a crash, Awake does so on its next launch.
- Removed the separate battery helper, which never worked on current macOS and was restarted every second while Keep Awake was on battery power.
- The helper has been updated. After installing this version, click **Update…** in the menu or in **Settings → Permissions**. Keep Awake with the lid closed stays unavailable until the helper is updated. Closed-lid operation also requires **Stay Awake with the Lid Closed** in **Settings → Keep Awake**.

This release also builds on [Beta 1](https://github.com/PMGWork/awake/releases/tag/v0.6.2-beta.1) and [Beta 2](https://github.com/PMGWork/awake/releases/tag/v0.6.2-beta.2).

## Installation

Download `Awake-0.6.2-beta.3.zip` or `Awake-0.6.2-beta.3.dmg` and move `Awake.app` to `/Applications`. Existing users with beta updates enabled can use **Settings → General → Check Now**. The build is not notarized; on first launch, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** if macOS blocks it.

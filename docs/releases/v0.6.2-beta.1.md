This is a beta release. To receive it in-app, enable **Settings → General → Receive Beta Updates**.

## What's Changed Since 0.6.1

- Fixed the Mac sometimes going to sleep when the lid was closed during Keep Awake, most often after switching between AC and battery power or after waking from sleep. macOS can reset the system-wide lid-close sleep setting at these times, so the helper now reapplies the setting every 0.5 seconds while it keeps the Mac awake with the lid closed.
- The helper has been updated. After installing this version, open **Settings → Permissions** and click **Update…** to authorize the new helper. Keep Awake with the lid closed stays unavailable until the helper is updated.

## Installation

Download `Awake-0.6.2-beta.1.zip` or `Awake-0.6.2-beta.1.dmg` and move `Awake.app` to `/Applications`. Existing users with beta updates enabled can use **Settings → General → Check Now**. The build is not notarized; on first launch, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** if macOS blocks it.
